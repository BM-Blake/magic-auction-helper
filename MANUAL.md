# User manual

Fan-made guide for Magic Auction Helper. The same sections are in the app: on the menu, tap the book in the top-right corner of the screen.

## Start a game

Choose a hero and an auction house, then tap Game Start. End game saves the round to Match history when it has treasures or a total, then clears the treasures and keeps your hero and house.

## Adding treasures

Tap + to add information. Known count adds that many treasures. Open rarity, type, or size and use the up and down arrows beside each count to split that count across different values, the same way the information card does. Max replaces that category's maximum. A lower maximum removes treasures that have only that category until the listed count matches. It is refused when a treasure that already has another property would have to be removed. Both replaces the listed count and the maximum with the new number, using that same rule. A maximum with no rarity, type, or size sets the treasure total and keeps Max on. You cannot add treasures past that total. Max beside Total treasures saves the current count as the treasure total, and tapping it again clears that one.

## Heroes

### Johnson

The first time you add information as Johnson, gray, green, and blue share one card. Each number is how many there are, and their maximum. You can remove that card.

## Read what you have

A chip appears when that rarity, type, or size has a listed treasure, or a maximum above zero. Tap it to open those treasures. With two or more, the other categories stay available above the list. One treasure opens on its own. A count shows as listed/maximum. Unknown holds treasures that are still missing that fact. You cannot add more of a category than its maximum.

## Worth and bids

Estimate worth is a range from the treasure catalog, and its top stops at the house's usual value for a whole pool. Safe bet is the cheapest the whole pool can be, so winning at or under it can't lose coins. It gives every listed treasure, every treasure a color maximum still needs, and the rest of the treasure total the cheapest catalog treasure each can still be. Each catalog treasure is used once. Calculated risk and Never more than are 80% of a worth where known prices count in full. Every other treasure adds half the average price of what can still fill its slot for Calculated risk, and the average price for Never more than. Listed treasures that already fill a maximum do not raise that price. A maximum with nothing listed still leaves that color, type, or size out. A listed treasure with no color fills a color that has a maximum first, cheapest color first. A remainder that can only be gold or red uses half the average of every gold and red treasure for Calculated risk, and the average for Never more than. Anything left with more than one open color uses the sum of each open color's price, divided by the weights gray 32, green 16, blue 8, purple 4, gold 2, and red 1. A color, type, or size that cannot appear is left out. That worth stops at the house's usual pool value, and neither price goes below Safe bet. They stay at 0 until this game has a treasure, a total, or a maximum. For a house with logged match totals, before this game's treasure total is known, all three prices use the lowest logged count.

## Match history

Match history keeps each finished round on this device, including the recommendation prices. End game asks for the pool value, the winning bid, and whether you won, and each one is optional. With a pool value, each price shows the profit or loss of winning at that price. Remove drops one round from the history.

## Database

Database lists the catalog. Search by treasure name, and filter by rarity, type, and size. Tap a filter to open it, and tap the same choice again to clear it.

Your game stays on this device. After the first online visit, the page keeps working offline.
