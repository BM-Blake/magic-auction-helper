# User manual

Fan-made guide for Magic Auction Helper. The same sections are in the app: on the menu, tap the book in the top-right corner of the screen.

## Start a game

Choose a hero and an auction house, then tap Game Start. End game clears this round’s treasures and keeps your hero and house.

## Adding treasures

Tap + to add information. Each count adds that many treasures. Known count records treasures you have seen. Max sets a maximum. Both uses the same number for the count and the maximum. A maximum with no rarity, type, or size sets the treasure total.

## Heroes

### Johnson

The first time you add information as Johnson, gray, green, and blue share one card. Each number is how many there are, and their maximum. You can remove that card.

## Read what you have

A chip appears when that rarity, type, or size has a count. Tap it to open those treasures. A count shows as listed/maximum. Unknown holds treasures that are still missing that fact. You cannot add more of a category than its maximum.

## Worth and bids

Estimate worth is a range from the treasure catalog. Highest profit is 80% of the estimated worth. Calculated risk is 1.3 times that bid, and Never more than is 1.5 times that bid. In Eruditio, before the treasure total is known, those three prices are 80% of the worth at 10, 17, and 24 treasures.

## Database

Database lists the catalog. Search by treasure name, and filter by rarity, type, and size. Tap a filter to open it, and tap the same choice again to clear it.

Your game stays on this device. After the first online visit, the page keeps working offline.
