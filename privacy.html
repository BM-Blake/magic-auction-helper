Privacy policy

Magic Auction Helper. Last updated November 12, 2026.

Magic Auction Helper is a tracker for treasures you have already revealed in Magic Auction. It does not ask you to create an account.

What stays on your device

The hero, auction house, revealed treasures, prices, estimates, and match history are stored only on the device, in the app's local storage. That information is not sent to the developer and is not uploaded to a server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage, removes it.

What the app includes

The Android app contains its own copy of the treasure list, prices, and shapes so it can work without opening the website. The website version can download those same files from this site when a connection is available, and keeps a copy on the device for offline use.

Visit count

When this page is opened online, it sends a visit to GoatCounter so the developer can see how many people open it. The visit includes the page address, the referrer, the browser, the screen size, and a rough location. It does not include the hero, treasures, prices, or match history. GoatCounter does not set a cookie for this count. Offline opens are not counted. Their policy is at goatcounter.com/privacy.

Optional links

Buy Me a Coffee and Donate with Crypto are optional. If you choose one, your browser opens that service or shows a wallet address for you to copy. Those services have their own policies. The app does not receive payment information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact address on the Google Play listing, or through github.com/BM-Blake/magic-auction-helper.
